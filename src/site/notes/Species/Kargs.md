**Kargs** are monstrous beings that seek only to destroy. They first appeared during the [[First Wave\|First Wave]], ending the Ancient War. Since then, there has not been a wave as great as the First Wave, but recently, their numbers have been increasing, so some warn of another wave.

While vaguely human-like in appearance, they could not be farther from humans. Most have several rows of sharp teeth, and most walk on all fours. 
Kargs have thick leathery skin, though it is sometimes hidden beneath fur or scales. They have glowing green eyes, and from the few experiments that were done, they have no soul, and most don't have a mind either, but all of them have an identical spirit. 

## Karg Classes
The [[Adventurer's Guild\|Adventurer's Guild]] classifies Kargs into a few classes. These classes are based on both danger level and abilities.
The name of a class is made by combining the danger level class and ability class. The danger levels go up from A, where A is about as dangerous as a regular wolf, and G is capable of destroying entire cities on its own.
