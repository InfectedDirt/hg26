A **spell**, also known as a **miracle**, is an act of enacting your will on reality.
In essence, a mage convinces reality, or rather the [[Weave\|Weave]], to change. Any violation of the laws of the universe needs to be fueled through the [[Magic/Pathway\|Pathway]] of the caster. 
Mages therefore try to minimize how much their spells violate the laws of the universe, as overuse can lead to Dissolution.
For example, to make fire out of thin air, one would have to supply all energy through the Pathway. In this case, it would be easier to carry a combustible or flammable substance to fuel the fire.
