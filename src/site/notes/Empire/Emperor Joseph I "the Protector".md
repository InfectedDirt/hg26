**Emperor Joseph I "the Protector"** of the [[Tahlinger\|Tahlinger]] dynasty was the second Emperor of the Living. He is known for building the [[Wall\|Wall]]. 
After the death of his uncle, [[Empire/Emperor Karl I\|Emperor Karl I]], to a [[Karg\|Karg]] attack during the surveying of the borders of the [[Empire\|Empire]], Joseph I was appointed Emperor by the newly established council. 
At the young age of 17, as the only living relative of Karl I, he inherited a fragile Empire even younger than himself, and where Karl I was a conqueror and uniter, Joseph I was a diplomat. He quickly established his position as Emperor by founding the Border Patrol, which would later be known as 
the [[Guards of the Wall\|Guards of the Wall]]. 
He also enlarged the council, which previously consisted of just three seats for the three kings, to also include seats for a few of the more powerful lords.

In the year 6 AU he married [[Maria Frank\|Maria Frank]], daughter of [[King Albrecht I\|King Albrecht I]], strengthening his bond with the [[Eastern Kingdom\|Eastern Kingdom]]. 
They got three children, two sons and a daughter. 

Also in 6 AU, Joseph I started work on the Wall, a massive project that would take the rest of his reign to complete. 
At the opening of the Wall in 25 AU, he was killed by an Elven assassin[^1], who was never caught.
