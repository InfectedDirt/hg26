**Emperor Karl I**, of the [[Tahlinger\|Tahlinger]] dynasty, was the first Emperor of the Living, after uniting the [[Empire\|Empire]] in his 20 year long campaign.
He died in 3 AU from a [[Karg\|Karg]] attack while he was surveying the borders of his empire.
